authentik Enterprise adds features for identity provisioning, security, compliance, and device access to your self-hosted installation. Enable them by installing a license key. Enterprise uses the same container images and upgrade process as the open source version.

Licenses are validated locally, so Enterprise also works in [air-gapped environments](../install-config/air-gapped.mdx#enterprise-licenses).

## Set up and manage Enterprise

- [Get started](./get-started.mdx) to obtain and install a license on a new or existing installation.
- [Explore Enterprise features](./enterprise-features.mdx) and follow their configuration guides.
- [Manage licenses and billing](./manage-enterprise.mdx) to check user capacity, replace a license key, or manage your Customer Portal organization.
- [Get Enterprise support](./enterprise-support.mdx) to open a ticket and share diagnostic information.

## Choose a plan

Enterprise and Enterprise Plus include the same product features. Support and purchase options depend on your plan and subscription; see [plan details](./enterprise-features.mdx#enterprise-and-enterprise-plus).
